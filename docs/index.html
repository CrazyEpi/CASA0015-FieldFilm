FieldFilm is the ultimate IoT companion app for film photographers. Log your location, weather, altitude, and voice memos with a single tap. Never lose the context of your analog shots again.

See It In Action

The Analog Dilemma

Digital cameras save EXIF data (location, time, settings) automatically. But when shooting on chemical film, photographers are forced to carry bulky notebooks, slowing down their creative workflow. Without accurate real-time environmental data like temperature and altitude, it's impossible to learn from past mistakes during the film development process.

1-Tap Context Sensing

Instantly pulls precise GPS coordinates, gets your current location, and fetches real-time OpenWeather data including temperature and conditions.

Voice Memos

Keep your eyes on the viewfinder. Record audio memos to capture your thoughts, lighting conditions, and exposure settings hands-free.

Darkroom Aesthetic UI

A beautifully crafted interface featuring film inspired designs, fluid horizontal swiping, and based in dark mode optimized for outdoor visibility.

Serverless Cloud Sync

Powered by Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Your logs are securely backed up in the cloud and accessible across all your devices.
